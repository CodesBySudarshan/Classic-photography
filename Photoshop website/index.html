Meet The Owner

The Vision Behind The Lens: Rahul Rathod

At Classic Photography, owner and lead photographer Rahul Rathod approaches every shoot as an indelible piece of living art. From the emotional intimacy of royal Indian weddings to high-fashion editorial portfolios and soulful family milestones, Rahul's vision blends cinematic storytelling with authentic human connection.

Under Rahul's artistic direction, Classic Photography operates its flagship studio at Karanja Road, Pingle Gali, Beed, Maharashtra, equipped with cinema-grade optics, master lighting, and a dedicated creative team traveling across Maharashtra and India for bespoke shoots.
